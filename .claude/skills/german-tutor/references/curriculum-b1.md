# Curriculum B1 — canonical topic key registry

The grammar inventory between A2.2 and Goethe-Zertifikat B1. Same permanence
rules as `curriculum-a2.md`: the `Key` column is an identity and is never
renamed. Keys here are bare (no prefix), matching the A2.1/A2.2 style. The `A1_`
prefix exists only on legacy Band 1–2 keys.

None of these keys carry history yet. They are safe to rename **until** they are
written into `grammar.json` for the first time.

A key belongs here rather than in `curriculum-a2.md` when the learner is expected
to *produce* it at B1. Several structures appear receptively at A2.2 and
productively at B1 — those keep their A2 key and their A2 schedule. Do not create
a `_B1` twin of an existing key.

## Grammar

### Verb system

| Key | Topic |
|-----|-------|
| `Konjunktiv2_Irreal` | Unreal conditions: wenn ich Zeit hätte, würde ich … |
| `Konjunktiv2_Vergangenheit` | Past unreal: hätte gemacht, wäre gegangen, hätte kommen können |
| `Passiv_Vergangenheit` | Passive in the past: wurde gebaut, ist gebaut worden |
| `Passiv_Modalverben` | Passive with modals: muss repariert werden |
| `Passiv_Alternativen` | man, sich lassen, sein + zu as passive substitutes |
| `Futur_I` | werden + Infinitiv for prediction and firm intention |
| `Tempusgebrauch` | When to use Perfekt vs. Präteritum — spoken vs. written register |
| `Modalverben_Subjektiv` | Modals expressing certainty: er muss krank sein, sie dürfte recht haben |

## Sequencing note

Do not teach this list in order. The queue in `SKILL.md` decides what is served;
this file only says what exists and what a key is called.

That said, when introducing new B1 material and several keys are equally
available, prefer in this order:

1. Structures that unblock the Goethe Schreiben and Sprechen tasks —
   `Konjunktiv2_Irreal`, `Konnektoren_Text`, `Zweiteilige_Konnektoren`,
   `Bedingungssaetze`. These are what the exam asks the learner to produce.
2. Structures that are high-frequency in reading and listening —
   `Passiv_Vergangenheit`, `Relativsatz_Praeposition`, `Praepositionen_Genitiv`.
3. Everything else.

`Modalverben_Subjektiv` and `Indirekte_Rede` with Konjunktiv I are the two
entries here that sit closest to B2. Introduce them for recognition only, and do
not grade production failures on them harshly — a 2 on those is not the same
signal as a 2 on `Relativsatz_Dativ`.
